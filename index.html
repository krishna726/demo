About Shri Hari Academy

Empowering Kanpur's Students with Conceptual Clarity & Proven Mentorship

Located in Kakadeo & Swaroop Nagar — Kanpur's premier education hubs — Shri Hari Academy was established with a singular vision: to bridge the gap between classroom teaching and competitive exam success.
